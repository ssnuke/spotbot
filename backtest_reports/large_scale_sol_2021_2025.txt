KEY FINDINGS

1. EVERY SINGLE ONE OF THE 5 FULL YEARS TESTED WAS PROFITABLE, AND TIGHTLY CLUSTERED
   2021: 77.29% return / 2.99% dd / PF 1.57
   2022: 60.21% return / 3.09% dd / PF 1.44  (the FTX/Luna crash year -- see note below)
   2023: 77.48% return / 3.92% dd / PF 1.58
   2024: 61.52% return / 2.79% dd / PF 1.47
   2025: 87.82% return / 2.63% dd / PF 1.72
   All 5 years land in a 60-88% return band with drawdown never exceeding 3.92%. This is a
   TIGHTER spread than the equivalent BTC report (which ranged 46-82% return, up to 4.96% dd),
   i.e. SOL under this strategy has been at least as consistent as BTC across full years, not
   just profitable in aggregate.

2. THE 2022 CRASH YEAR (WORST OF THE 5) STILL PRODUCED A SOLID RESULT
   2022 included both the Terra/Luna collapse (May) and the FTX collapse (November) -- the
   latter hit SOL especially hard given Alameda/FTX's outsized SOL holdings, and SOL fell
   roughly 97% from its all-time high through this bear market. Despite that, 2022 is not an
   outlier low in this dataset -- it's the weakest of the 5 years, but at 60.21% return and
   3.09% max drawdown it is still comfortably within the normal range of the other 4 years.
   This reinforces the standing finding from the BTC analysis: this strategy's danger zone is
   range-bound chop, not directional crashes -- a bidirectional trend-follower can profit from
   a crash (via short trades) as readily as a rally.

3. ROBUSTNESS ACROSS TIME-SLICING HOLDS FOR SOL TOO
   Win rate stays in a tight 57.2%-61.8% band and profit factor in a 1.43-1.73 band across all
   window granularities (30/90/180/365-day) and all 5 years -- the same robustness signature
   found in the BTC report. Negative-return windows: 3/60 at 30-day granularity (5%), just
   1/20 at 90-day (5%), and ZERO at 180-day and 365-day (0/10, 0/5). No quarter-or-longer slice
   of 5 years of SOL data has ever been net negative under the current shipped config.

4. THE TAKE-PROFIT-RATE CORRELATION REPLICATES ON A SECOND ASSET (r=0.782 vs BTC's r=0.812)
   The same diagnostic run on the BTC report -- correlating a window's take-profit exit rate
   with its return -- produces an almost identical result on SOL (r=0.782 here vs r=0.812 for
   BTC). This is a meaningful cross-asset replication: it suggests the "chop kills this
   strategy, trend feeds it" pattern is a property of the STRATEGY itself, not an artifact of
   BTC's specific price history. (Also consistent with the HTF trend filter rejection: filtering
   entries by higher-timeframe trend confirmation still doesn't work, per that separate test --
   the correlation is a real diagnostic signal, just not one a lagging filter can act on profitably.)

5. WORST 30-DAY WINDOW: OCT-NOV 2021 (-4.94%, win rate 40.5%, PF 0.66)
   This is the only 30-day window across all 5 years with a win rate below 45%. Not part of
   the 2022 crash narrative -- worth noting this was a genuinely difficult month independent
   of the well-known crash events, likely a chop/consolidation stretch.
