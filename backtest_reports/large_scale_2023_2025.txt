KEY FINDINGS
====================================================================================================

1. WIN RATE AND PROFIT FACTOR ARE STABLE ACROSS TIME-SLICING (strong robustness signal)
   Average win rate stays in a tight 56.6%-59.9% band whether you look at 30-day, 90-day,
   180-day, or full-year windows, across all three years. Average profit factor stays in a
   1.33-1.69 band the same way. This is NOT an artifact of picking a lucky aggregation window
   -- the edge shows up consistently no matter how the calendar is sliced.

2. NEGATIVE-RETURN WINDOWS ARE RARE AND SELF-CORRECT WITHIN A QUARTER
   Only 2 of 36 independent 30-day windows were net negative (both in 2024). At 90-day
   granularity and above, ZERO of the 19 windows across all three years were negative --
   every quarter-or-longer slice of the last 3 years of BTC data was profitable under the
   current shipped config. Max drawdown never exceeded 5.46% in any window of any size.

3. THE CLEAREST LEVER: TAKE-PROFIT RATE PREDICTS WINDOW QUALITY (r=0.812)
   Correlation between a window's take-profit exit rate and its return, across all 36
   30-day windows: r=0.812 (strong). Bottom-quartile TP-rate windows (avg 9.0% of exits
   reaching target) averaged only 2.12% return and PF 1.24. Top-quartile TP-rate windows
   (avg 21.7% reaching target) averaged 9.54% return and PF 2.01 -- more than 4x the return
   at roughly the same win rate. In other words: the single biggest driver of a good period
   isn't "more wins," it's "wins that reach the full 3% target instead of getting trailed out
   early." This points squarely at a TREND-STRENGTH / REGIME FILTER as the next highest-value
   change: something that detects when price action isn't trending cleanly (visible here as
   low TP%/high trailing-stop% before the fact would require a live regime signal, not just
   hindsight) and reduces trading during those stretches, rather than taking the same signal
   quality regardless of regime.

4. 2024 IS THE WEAK YEAR, AND IT'S CONCENTRATED, NOT SPREAD OUT
   2024 full-year return (46.36%) trails 2023 (82.17%) and 2025 (56.89%). Both of the only
   two negative 30-day windows across all 3 years are in 2024 (Jan: -0.16%, Aug-Sep: -2.57%),
   and both have unusually low TP% (8.3% each, vs the 36-window average of ~16%). This is
   consistent with earlier findings in this project that choppy/range-bound conditions (not
   downtrends -- 2022's crash year performed fine) are this strategy's actual weak point.

5. SCOPE LIMITATION: THIS TEST IS BTC/USDT ONLY
   Multi-year (2023-2025) 5m history was only available for BTC/USDT. The live bot's
   configured symbol is currently SOL/USDT, which has only been validated on a ~2-month
   2026 window earlier in this project. This report's robustness conclusions do not
   automatically transfer to SOL -- if long-horizon confidence on SOL specifically is
   wanted, that requires pulling multi-year SOL history (availability/liquidity history
   depth would need to be checked) and re-running an equivalent sweep.
